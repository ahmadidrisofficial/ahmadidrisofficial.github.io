Why Pathways exists

Careers change in rooms. A talk you nearly skipped, a workshop that reframes a problem, a person you meet in the tea queue. But the best rooms are badly advertised, and the people who would gain most from them often hear about them last.

Pathways is my attempt to fix a small corner of that. Each week I pick out events I would actually recommend, say plainly why each one matters and who it is for, and add a short note on what I have been up to. If you attend something I flagged, write and tell me how it went. This is a community I am building on purpose, and it starts with people talking to each other.

Edition one: dates for your diary

Published Friday 28 August 2026. Details come from each event's official pages; always confirm dates and tickets there.

EdTech Summit, NEC Birmingham

18 and 19 November 2026. The Schools and Academies Show's dedicated EdTech event, aimed at school and trust leaders deciding what technology actually earns its place in classrooms. If you build or buy education technology in the UK, this is a practical room rather than a showcase. Details and registration (opens in a new tab)

Bett UK 2027, ExCeL London

20 to 22 January 2027. The largest education technology gathering in the world, and worth planning months ahead: the value is in the meetings you book before you arrive, not the stands you wander past. Free tickets for educators are usually released in the autumn. Bett UK official site (opens in a new tab)

Black Tech Fest, London

The UK's largest celebration of Black talent and innovation in technology, usually held in October during Black History Month. A strong room for founders and operators from the African diaspora, including many Nigerian builders. Dates and tickets are announced on the official site. Black Tech Fest (opens in a new tab)

Impact Investing Institute briefings

The Institute runs a rolling programme of briefings and roundtables on directing capital towards measurable social good, many of them free and online. A good low-commitment way into the UK impact investment conversation. Upcoming events (opens in a new tab)

Good Finance events for social organisations

Free workshops and drop-ins on social investment, designed for charities and social enterprises considering repayable finance. If you run a social organisation and the word investment makes you nervous, start here. Events calendar (opens in a new tab)
